InneRISE

Privacy Policy

Effective November 14, 2026 · InneRISE is made by Shortbus Design Group, LLC ("we", "us")

InneRISE helps you use your phone on purpose. Some features work between people (your Anchors, your connections, or a parent and child), so they need an account and our server. This policy explains what InneRISE collects, why, who can see it, how long we keep it, and how to delete it.

The short version:

  • Apple's Screen Time keeps your app use on your phone. InneRISE never receives the names of the apps you use, the websites you visit, or how many minutes you spend on them.
  • Our server stores only what features need to work between people, such as your first name, your Anchor requests, your challenges and, for Family Protection, whether a child's protections are working.
  • If you join activity challenges, InneRISE uses your Apple Health, Garmin, Ride with GPS or Fitbit activity only to count it in those challenges. We never sell or monetize it, use it for ads, or use it for AI (section 6).
  • We don't sell your information, show ads, or use analytics or tracking tools.
  • You can delete your account in the app at any time.

1. Your account

You sign in with Apple, or with your email address and a one-time code. Some accounts have no sign-in method yet, for example ones made with earlier test versions. They work the same way, and you can add Apple or email sign-in to them.

  • Sign in with Apple: Apple gives us an ID for your account and, if Apple shares it, your email address. That may be a private relay address that forwards to you. If you share your name, InneRISE keeps your first name on your phone.
  • Email sign-in: we store your email address and email you sign-in codes.
  • Your first name is shared with the people you choose to involve when you use My Anchor, Connections, Challenges or Family Protection. If someone finds you by your phone number, email address, name or InneRISE code (section 4), they see your first name, never your photo.

We use your account to keep those features working, including when you move to a new phone.

2. Screen Time, and what stays on your phone

InneRISE uses Apple's Screen Time for app limits, Night Mode, bedtime, lockouts and Family Protection. Apple keeps this information on your phone. InneRISE only sees Apple's private stand-ins for the apps you pick, never their names, and the screen time totals shown in the app are drawn by Apple on your phone. Two results based on Screen Time do leave your phone:

  • In a verified challenge, whether you met each day's goal (kept or missed). People in that challenge can see it.
  • On a child's phone with Family Protection, whether its protections are on and working (see section 5).

These stay only on your phone, and in your own iPhone backups if you have backups turned on. They don't move to a new phone when you sign in there.

  • Goals, streaks, badges and reminders.
  • Inspiration: notes, links, photos, videos and voice notes, and your music lineups.
  • Morning Rise alarms, Night Mode notes and priorities, your intention and the good things you write down.
  • Scroll Guard and Content Guard settings. Content Guard uses Apple's website filter on your phone and doesn't read the pages you visit.
  • Your profile photo.
  • Your Anchor's unlock code, stored only in scrambled (hashed) form.

InneRISE asks for these permissions only when a feature needs them:

4. Contacts, phone numbers and finding people

  • Adding your phone number is optional. Our server turns it into a fingerprint with a secret key and stores only that fingerprint and the last two digits, not the number. People who have your number can then find you and see your first name, unless you turn off "People who have my number can find me". You can remove your number at any time.
  • Finding someone: when you pick a contact, InneRISE sends that contact's phone numbers and a scrambled (hashed) form of their email addresses, to check whether they use InneRISE. Nothing about the contact is stored, and your address book is never uploaded.
  • Being found by email: someone who has your email address in a contact can find your Connections profile and see your first name, and can invite you.
  • Being found by name (off unless you turn it on): if you turn on "Let people find me by name" in Connections → Your Profile, other InneRISE users can find you by typing at least the first three letters of your first name. They see only your first name. You can turn it off at any time.
  • Your InneRISE code: anyone you give your code to can use it to find you and see your first name, even when finding by name is off.
  • What people who find you see: only your first name, however they find you. Your profile photo stays on your phone and is never shown to anyone else.
  • Invitations from people who find you: someone who finds you by name, code, phone number or email can invite you to a challenge. The invitation shows their first name and the challenge title, and you can accept or decline. Accepting doesn't make you connections. People you've blocked, or who have blocked you, can't find or invite you.
  • Children: a child's account can't be found by name, phone number, email address or InneRISE code by anyone who isn't already one of the child's connections. To anyone else, it looks the same as no account, and no request reaches the child. A child's existing connections can still find them, a child can still send their own connection requests, and only a child's own connections can invite them to a challenge. Children can't search for people or look up codes.

5. Family Protection and children

Family Protection lets a parent or guardian set limits on a child's iPhone. The parent sets it up on the child's phone, using Apple Family Sharing and a parent passcode. For a child's account we store:

  • the child's email address, entered by the parent, which the child uses to sign in;
  • the child's first name, if the parent adds one;
  • the phone's model (for example "iPhone 16") and a name the parent gives it;
  • regular check-ins: whether Screen Time permission, child mode, Family Sharing, the passcode and the limits are on and working, plus the app version and the time of the last check-in;
  • the limits the parent sets (daily minutes, bedtime and school hours) and extra time a parent approves;
  • requests for more time, parent approvals of a new sign-in, and a record when protection is switched off.

We never receive which apps a child uses or for how long, the websites they visit, or their location. When a parent resets the child's settings passcode from their own phone, the new passcode is locked so only the child's phone can open it. Our server holds only that locked version until it's used, and can't read it.

The child's linked parents can see this information. If a child's account is deleted, they see that it was deleted, with the child's name, until they dismiss it. If a child uses Connections or Challenges, sections 3 and 4 apply to them too.

InneRISE is meant for adults, and for children whose parent or guardian sets it up with Family Protection. We don't knowingly let a child under 13 create an account any other way. If you believe one has, contact us and we'll delete it.

6. Activity from Apple Health, Garmin, Ride with GPS and Fitbit

Activity challenges let you and the people you've connected with compete on steps, walking and running distance, cycling distance, floors climbed and active workout minutes. Connecting Apple Health, Garmin, Ride with GPS or Fitbit costs nothing, and taking part in an activity challenge you're invited to is free. We never charge for, sell or monetize your activity data.

Your permission

  • Apple Health: InneRISE asks Apple Health for permission when you first create or join an activity challenge, and reads only the kinds of data you allow. It never writes to Health. You can change or turn off access at any time in the Health app.
  • Garmin: nothing is received from Garmin unless you connect your Garmin account in InneRISE and approve it on Garmin's own page, where you choose what to share. You can disconnect at any time in InneRISE or in Garmin Connect.
  • Ride with GPS: nothing is received from Ride with GPS unless you connect your Ride with GPS account in InneRISE and approve it on Ride with GPS's own page. You can disconnect at any time in InneRISE or in your Ride with GPS settings.
  • Fitbit and Pixel Watch (Google Health): nothing is received from Google unless you connect your Google account in InneRISE and approve read-only access to your activity and fitness data on Google's own page. Before that page opens, InneRISE shows you what it will read and why. You can disconnect at any time in InneRISE or in your Google Account settings.

What we receive

  • From Apple Health: steps, walking and running distance, cycling distance, floors climbed and workouts (type, start and end, distance, pauses and elevation), with the app that recorded each one, device information when provided, and whether it was typed in by hand.
  • From Garmin: your daily steps, distance and floors, with whatever times Garmin provides for them, and your recorded activities (type, start and end, distance, pauses and elevation), with device information when provided and whether each was entered by hand.
  • From Ride with GPS: your trips (type, start, length, distance and elevation gain), whether a trip was edited by hand, and device information when provided. Ride with GPS sends the trip's GPS track with it; our server only checks whether the track was recorded or edited by hand, and never stores the route or GPS points.
  • From Fitbit and Pixel Watch (Google Health): steps, walking and running distance, floors climbed and workouts (type, start and end, distance and elevation), with how each was recorded (measured by a device, worked out from a device's data, or typed in), which service wrote it into Google Health, and device information when provided. We don't read your heart rate, sleep, location or GPS routes, or your Google profile.
  • We keep only the days inside the challenges you've joined. We never ask for routes or GPS tracks, heart rate, sleep, stress, women's health or medical records.

How we use it

  • Only to count your activity in the challenges you join: our server checks that each record was recorded by a device and not typed in, counts activity once when two devices recorded the same thing, and works out your daily result, total or points, and place.
  • People in the same challenge see your progress, totals, points and standings, according to your privacy settings: if you keep your progress private, they see only your place. Your individual activity records, and the apps and devices that recorded them, are never shown to anyone else.

Where it's kept, and who receives it

  • Supabase is our hosting provider. Your activity data is stored in our database on Supabase's servers, which Supabase runs for us. Supabase handles it only to provide that hosting.
  • Apple delivers challenge notifications, which can include first names and challenge results.
  • Garmin, Ride with GPS and Google receive only InneRISE's request to stop sharing with us when you disconnect them or delete your account. We send them none of your activity.
  • We don't share it with anyone else: no integration partners, data aggregators, advertisers, or AI or data-processing services, except where the law requires it.

What we never do with it

  • We never sell, rent or monetize Apple Health, Garmin, Ride with GPS or Fitbit data, or use or share it for advertising or marketing.
  • No AI system processes it, and it's never used to train, evaluate or improve AI models.

The use of information received from Google Health API and/or Developer Tools will adhere to the Google Health API Developer and User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

How long we keep it, and deleting it

  • A daily cleanup deletes your activity records and daily challenge totals once 90 days have passed since the challenge they were used for ended. Your final place and total stay in your challenge history until you delete your account.
  • When you disconnect Garmin, Ride with GPS or Fitbit, the activity records we hold from that service are deleted as part of disconnecting, and challenges still running are recounted without them. We also ask that service to stop sharing with us; if it can't be reached, your records are still deleted. Results of challenges that have already finished stay in your challenge history.
  • Deleting your account deletes all of your activity records, connections and challenge history, and we ask each connected service to stop sharing with us.
  • Copies in our hosting provider's backups are removed when those backups expire.
  • Turning off Apple Health access or disconnecting Garmin, Ride with GPS or Fitbit stops new data from being saved. Records already saved from Apple Health stay until the daily cleanup above, or until you ask us to delete them. You can email us at any time for a copy of your activity data, to correct it, or to delete it; we handle these requests by hand.

7. Notifications

Alerts about Anchor requests, connections and family are delivered by Apple's push notification service. Their text can include first names and messages, for example "Sam asks to unlock". Reminders and alarms are scheduled on your phone.

8. Subscriptions and payments

Purchases are made through Apple. We never see your card or payment details. Apple sends us a signed record of each purchase, which we check and store with your account (section 3) so your plan works. We include your InneRISE account ID with the purchase so Apple's record and your account match. You can manage or cancel a subscription in iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

9. Services InneRISE uses

  • Supabase runs our server and database. It may keep short-term technical logs, such as IP addresses, as part of running the service.
  • Apple provides Sign in with Apple, push notifications, App Store purchases, Screen Time, Apple Health and Apple Music.
  • Garmin, if you connect your Garmin account, sends your activity to our server (section 6). Garmin's own privacy policy applies to your Garmin account.
  • Ride with GPS, if you connect your Ride with GPS account, lets our server read your trips (section 6). Ride with GPS's own privacy policy applies to your Ride with GPS account.
  • Google, if you connect Fitbit or Pixel Watch through Google Health, lets our server read your activity (section 6). Google's own privacy policy applies to your Google account.
  • An email delivery service sends sign-in codes and receives your email address to do so.
  • Links and music you add: link previews, YouTube thumbnails, Apple Music search and players from YouTube, Instagram, Spotify or SoundCloud connect your phone directly to those services. They have their own privacy policies.
  • Messages and Mail: invitations and "Share my week" open Messages, Mail or the share sheet. You choose who receives them, and we don't get a copy.

We share your information only as described here, or when the law requires it.

10. What we don't do

  • We don't sell or rent your information.
  • We don't use advertising, analytics or tracking tools, or work with data brokers.
  • We don't use Screen Time information for marketing or advertising.
  • We don't sell or monetize Apple Health, Garmin, Ride with GPS or Fitbit data, or use it for advertising or AI (section 6).

11. How long we keep it, and deleting your account

We keep your information while your account is open. Anchor invitation codes and Family Protection link codes stop working after an hour, and a passcode reset is held for up to 24 hours.

To delete your account: Protection → Account → Delete Account. If you use Sign in with Apple, you confirm with Apple, and we revoke InneRISE's Sign in with Apple access. Choose Delete Account to keep what's on your phone (goals, Inspiration, alarms), or Delete Account & Local Data to erase those too. Screen Time limits and Family Protection on the phone stay in place either way. On a child's phone, deleting needs the parent passcode.

Deleting removes your account and the information tied to it from our server, except what other people still need:

  • unlock requests you answered for someone else stay in their history, shown as "A former Anchor";
  • groups and challenges you created pass to another member, with their names, titles and prizes;
  • other people's inbox messages keep their text, with your name replaced by "A former member";
  • a child's linked parents see that the account was deleted, as described in section 5;
  • purchase records stay, no longer linked to you.

Deleting the app alone doesn't delete your account. Your sign-in stays in the iPhone's Keychain so you can get back in.

12. Security

  • Connections between the app and our server are encrypted.
  • Access rules on our database limit each person to what's described in this policy.
  • Sign-in keys are kept in the iPhone's Keychain.
  • Unlock codes and settings passcodes are stored only in hashed form, phone numbers only as fingerprints, and passcode resets locked for the child's phone.

13. Your choices and rights

  • Change your name, your phone number, whether people can find you by your number, and whether people can find you by name, in the app.
  • Turn off notifications or Screen Time access at any time in iPhone Settings.
  • Delete your account in the app (section 11).
  • Email us to ask for a copy of what our server holds about you, or to correct or delete it. We'll reply within 30 days. Depending on where you live, you may have further rights, and you can use them the same way.

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top and describe important changes in the app's release notes.
