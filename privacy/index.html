Privacy Policy

Effective [DATE OF PUBLISHING] · InneRISE is made by Shortbus Design Group, LLC ("we", "us")

InneRISE helps you use your phone on purpose. Most of what you do in InneRISE stays on your iPhone. When you use features that involve other people (an InneRISE account, My Anchor, Connections, Challenges and Family Protection), the information those features need is stored on our server so the right people can see it. We don't sell your data, show ads, or use analytics or tracking tools. We can't see which apps you use or how long you use them.

Screen Time: what we can and can't see

InneRISE uses Apple's Screen Time system for app limits, Night Mode, lockouts, Scroll Guard, Content Guard and screen-time reports. Apple keeps this information on your device. InneRISE only receives Apple's private stand-ins for the apps and websites you choose, so we can't see their names, and your screen-time totals are shown to you by Apple on your phone. None of this is sent to our server.

Two things related to Screen Time can leave your phone, and only when you use these features:

  • Challenges with other people: for each day, whether you kept the challenge (yes or no), and any number you enter yourself, such as minutes saved. Your screen-time details are never sent.
  • Family Protection on a child's phone: whether protection is working (see "Family Protection" below). Never which apps the child uses or for how long.

Your InneRISE account

You sign in with Sign in with Apple or with your email address.

  • Sign in with Apple: Apple gives us an account identifier and an email address. If you choose "Hide My Email", it's a private relay address from Apple. We don't receive your Apple password.
  • Email: we store your email address and send you one-time sign-in codes. These emails are sent through an email delivery service we use for this purpose only.
  • Earlier test versions of InneRISE could create an account without a name or email. These accounts hold the same kinds of information described here and can be deleted the same way.

Finding friends from your contacts

When you pick a contact to find on InneRISE, only that one contact is checked. InneRISE never reads or uploads your address book. The contact's phone number is sent to our server to be compared with members' scrambled fingerprints, and their email address is sent only as a scrambled fingerprint. Neither is stored. A match shows only the person's first name and photo. Lookups are limited each day to prevent misuse.

Family Protection (parents and children)

Family Protection lets a parent or guardian set up and look after protection on a child's iPhone. It uses Apple's Screen Time with Family Sharing and a settings passcode the parent controls. When a child's phone is linked to a parent:

  • The child's phone reports whether protection is working: Screen Time permission, child mode, Family Sharing, settings passcode, limits set and running, the InneRISE version, and when it last checked in. We keep only the latest report for each phone. It doesn't include the child's apps, websites, messages, location or screen time.
  • What parents set is stored: the daily limit, bedtime and school hours they set from their phone. Which apps are restricted is chosen only on the child's phone, through Apple.
  • Requests and approvals: a child's requests for more time, and approvals when the child's account signs in on a new device.
  • Settings passcode resets: a parent can reset the passcode from their phone. The new passcode is sealed so that only the child's phone can open it; our server can't read it.
  • The phone's name and an InneRISE device number: created by the app, not a hardware identifier.

A child's account information (name, sign-in email, the data above) is visible to the linked parents. Parents can rename, unlink or remove a child's phone at any time from their dashboard. If you're a parent and want to review or delete your child's information, contact us (below).

What stays on your phone

  • Your settings, goals, streaks, badges, rewards and reminders.
  • Your Inspiration and Lineups: notes, links, photos, videos, voice notes and songs you save.
  • Your intention, priorities, journal entries and the good things you write down.
  • Your Morning Rise alarms and Night Mode schedule.
  • Your settings passcode, stored only in scrambled (hashed) form.

This information isn't sent to us and doesn't move to a new phone when you sign in there. It may be included in your own iCloud or computer backup if you have backups turned on.

Subscriptions and payments

Purchases are handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details. Apple tells us which plan you bought and whether it's active, renewed, refunded or expired, so InneRISE can unlock the right features. You can manage or cancel your subscription in your iPhone's Settings under your Apple Account → Subscriptions.

Who we share information with

We don't sell or rent your information, and we don't share it for advertising. Your information is shared only:

  • With the people you choose: your Anchors, connections, group and challenge members, and linked parents, as described above.
  • With the services that run InneRISE:
    • Supabase hosts our database and server.
    • Apple provides Sign in with Apple, push notifications and the App Store.
    • An email delivery service sends sign-in codes.
    They process information only to provide these services to us.
  • If required by law, or to protect the safety of our users.

Some features open Apple's Messages app so you can send a text yourself, such as an invitation. You choose who receives it and what is sent; we don't receive a copy. Links you open from your Inspiration open in a browser view inside InneRISE, and those websites have their own privacy policies.

Keeping information secure

  • Information sent between InneRISE and our server is encrypted in transit.
  • Database access rules limit each account to the information its features allow.
  • Your sign-in details on your phone are kept in Apple's secure Keychain.
  • Phone numbers are stored only as keyed fingerprints.
  • Passcodes are stored only in hashed form.

No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and fix problems promptly.

How long we keep information

We keep your account information for as long as you have an account. When you delete it, it's removed from our server, except:

  • Things you wrote in shared spaces stay for the other people in them: group names, challenge names, prizes and results. Your name is removed from them, and your encouragement messages are deleted.
  • A child's phone stays on the parent's dashboard marked "Account deleted", so the parent knows protection may have changed.
  • Unlock answers you gave as an Anchor stay in the other person's history as "A former Anchor".
  • Subscription records are kept without your account attached, as needed for the App Store's purchase history.

Our service providers may keep technical logs for a limited time.

Deleting your account and your rights

  • Delete your account in the app: Protection → Account → Delete Account. This deletes your account and its information from our server. If you used Sign in with Apple, it also cancels InneRISE's access to your Apple sign-in. You can also choose to erase InneRISE's data from your phone at the same time.
  • A child's account can be deleted from the child's phone with a parent's approval.
  • Deleting the app removes everything InneRISE stored on your phone, but not your account. Delete your account first if you want it gone.
  • You can always: change your name and photo, turn off phone-number discovery, remove connections and Anchors, and turn off InneRISE's Screen Time access in iPhone Settings → Screen Time.
  • To ask for a copy of your information or to correct it, email us. Depending on where you live, you may have further rights over your personal information, and we'll respond to requests as the law requires.

Children

InneRISE is for adults managing their own phone use, and for parents or guardians who set it up on a child's iPhone. A child's InneRISE account should be set up with a parent or guardian, who links it to their own account and can see and manage it as described above. If you believe a child has given us information without a parent's involvement, contact us and we'll delete it.
