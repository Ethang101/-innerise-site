Challenges & Competitions

Class against class.
Grade against grade.

Run a challenge for one classroom, put grades or schools head to head, or bring the whole district together for a month.

  • Who takes part a classroom, a grade, a school or the whole district.
  • Teams, not rankings students win together as a class or a grade. No one is ranked against classmates.
  • What counts active days and steps, or days a teacher confirms.
  • Everyone can win rewards go to everyone who takes part, everyone who reaches the target, or the winning side.
Habits that count

Reading, kindness,
quiet time.

Not every good habit shows up on a watch. For reading, kindness or quiet time, the teacher taps who did it each day.

  • Teacher confirmed on the day or up to 3 days after. Students can't mark themselves.
  • Ready-made school challenges from the InneRISE library, like Class reading time and Active days.
  • Recognition certificates and badges, and the school approves every reward.

Parents in charge

Built for families first.

Students under 18 take part only when a parent or guardian sets them up with Family Protection.

  • Schools never see anyone's health records
  • Teachers see who took part and who reached the goal, nothing more
  • No weight, no body measurements, ever
  • Children can't be found by strangers
  • We don't sell information or use tracking tools
