Challenges

Beat your friends.
Beat your screen time.

Pick a challenge, invite your people, and InneRISE keeps score every day. Screen time challenges are checked by Screen Time on your phone. Step, distance and ride challenges count straight from your watch. Nobody types in a number, and nobody has to take anyone’s word for it.

  • Less scrolling. Scroll Less (stay under a daily limit you set, from 10 minutes to 4 hours), Slow Mornings (no scrolling before 9 AM), Screens Off by 10, and the Weekend Digital Reset.
  • More moving. Steps, walking and running distance, bike rides and floors climbed. Play for the most in total, or for a daily goal like 10,000 steps.
  • Better habits. Read Every Day, Daily Walk, Better Sleep, Phone-Free Dinner, Daily Gratitude, Date Night Unplugged and more. Answer one quick question a day to check in.
  • Your rules. 3, 7, 14 or 30 days or a length you pick, up to 10 people, and an optional “Winner gets” and “Loser has to,” like the loser buys the coffee.
  • A fair finish. One point for every day you make it. A tie goes to the longer best streak. Results lock in at noon the day after the last day, the same moment for everyone. Then tap Rematch.
  • Just you? Make your own challenge with up to 5 daily tasks and go it alone.
Fitness apps and wearables

Connect your watch.
Your steps count themselves.

Connect the fitness apps and watches you already use, and your activity flows into your challenges on its own. Only activity recorded by a device counts. Typed-in entries never do, so the standings stay fair.

  • Counts in challenges today. Apple Health (from your iPhone or Apple Watch), Garmin, and Fitbit or Pixel Watch.
  • Also connects. Ride with GPS, Polar, Withings and Oura show your rides, workouts and steps in the app. They don’t count in challenges yet. Oura needs an Oura membership.
  • Coming soon. Samsung Health and WHOOP.
  • Your data, your call. Disconnect an app whenever you want and InneRISE deletes everything it had from it. Activity from connected apps is deleted after 90 days.
Scroll Guard

Limits that
actually hold.

Pick the apps, categories and websites that pull you in most and give them a daily limit, anywhere from 5 minutes to 4 hours. You can also give one app its own limit, like 20 minutes of Instagram. A heads-up comes 1, 2, 5 or 10 minutes before time’s up. Then you choose what happens.

  • Pause and Reflect. Write down what you’re staying for and get 10 more minutes. Honest, not harsh.
  • Lock me out. The apps lock for 1, 2, 4 or 8 hours, the rest of the day, or 24 hours. Only your Anchor can open them early.
  • Change it later. Your limits and apps can be changed in the app any time you’re not locked. Lower limits start right away. Higher limits start tomorrow, so one weak moment can’t undo your plan.
  • Take a Break. Put the phone down for 15 minutes, 30 minutes, an hour or a length you pick, and end it early if you need to.
  • Private. Scroll Guard is built on Apple’s Screen Time, so what you use stays on your phone.
Night Mode and Morning Reset

Sleep on time.
Wake up on purpose.

From 10:30 PM to 6:30 AM, or the times you pick, your distracting apps rest. Before bed, write down up to three priorities for tomorrow. In the morning, Morning Reset opens with a one-minute breathing ring, last night’s priorities and an intention for the day. Tap Start my day and your apps are back.

  • A heads-up first. A notification lets you know before Night Mode starts.
  • Still up? Choose “I decide” for a 10-second breath and one honest question: is this worth your sleep? Or ask your Anchor for 15, 30 or 60 more minutes. Night Mode comes back by itself after.
  • Mornings that count. Start my day works from 4 AM to noon. Your priorities wait on Home, ready to check off.
Morning Rise

Wake up to your day,
not your feed.

Real alarms that ring even on silent and in Focus, with sounds that start soft and rise: morning chimes, gentle piano, kalimba, birdsong, soft rain, soft waves and more, or your own voice. When you stop the alarm, your morning starts with what you chose instead of whatever is in your feed.

  • After the alarm. A quiet moment with a greeting and a quote, a Lineup of your music with a photo slideshow, a saved song, video or voice note, or a calming sound on loop.
  • Your music. Apple Music (full songs for subscribers, 30-second previews for everyone else), songs in your library and audio you import play right inside the app.
  • Every alarm, your way. Repeat days or just once, snooze, a name, and a purpose: Morning Rise, Evening Reset, Motivation or My alarm.
  • One tap to start. Stop it before noon on your iPhone, Lock Screen or Apple Watch and your day starts. Morning Rise alarms need iOS 26 or later.
My Anchor

Backup for
your willpower.

An Anchor is someone you trust, like a partner, a friend or a parent. When you’re locked out and really need more time, tap Request More Time. They get a notification and answer Approve or Not now, from their phone or their Apple Watch. Add more than one and the first yes is enough.

  • Easy to add. Pick from your Connections, invite from your contacts (they get a text with a link), type their InneRISE code, or hand them your phone so they can set a secret code.
  • Requests that expire. A request lasts 15 minutes, with a countdown on their watch. After that you can ask again.
  • A yes resets your day. When your Anchor approves during the day, you get your normal daily time back.
  • Free to help. Being someone’s Anchor is always free.
Growth

Watch the small
steps add up.

Your goals, challenges, reminders and screen time, all in one place. Start with one of 21 ready-made goals across Mind, Body, Connection, Rest and Growth, or write your own. Each goal shows its streak, your last 7 days and how it’s going.

  • Tracked your way. By Screen Time, by Morning Reset, by checking it off, or by logging an amount.
  • Gentle reminders. Drink water, stretch, take a breath, look up, or write your own. They show on your Apple Watch too.
  • One good thing about today. A quick line to end the day on.
  • Badges you earn. Goal streaks of 7, 21, 30, 60 and 100 days, a lock kept its full time, seven Morning Resets and more.
Apple Watch

On your wrist.
Galaxy Watch next.

Check your place in a challenge, answer an Anchor request, or take a breath without reaching for your phone. That’s kind of the point.

  • Challenges. Your place, score and time left, plus the full standings.
  • My Anchor. Approve or Not now, with a 15-minute countdown ring. Parents pick the minutes for a child’s request.
  • Lift. Three guided breaths with gentle taps on your wrist.
  • Check-ins. Gentle taps at 10 AM, 1 PM, 4 PM and 7 PM, today’s intention, and your Night Mode status.
  • Samsung Galaxy Watch. A Galaxy Watch app is coming after the Android app.
Family Protection

Healthy limits for kids. Less arguing for everyone.

Set it up once on your child’s iPhone, then manage everything from your own phone. Linking is free.

  • A daily limit, bedtime and school hours your child can’t switch off. Calls and messages still work during school.
  • When your child asks for more time, you get a notification and pick the minutes, or Not now.
  • Your child can’t delete InneRISE, change its settings, or get around it by reinstalling.
  • See each phone’s status and last check-in, and share the job with a second parent.
