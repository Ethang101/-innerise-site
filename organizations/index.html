Same for every organization

Built the same way for everyone.

Whichever you pick, these parts work the same.

One dashboard

Run challenges, people and rewards on the web, or from the InneRISE app on your phone.

Verified first

We check every organization before it goes live, and review every sponsored challenge and reward.

Health stays private

No one’s health records are ever shared. You see only results people choose to share, and small groups are hidden.

Rewards, never cash

Things and experiences people earn, each code good once. No cash, gift cards or balances.

How it works

Up and running in three steps.

Register and get verified

Tell us who you are and who will manage InneRISE. We verify every organization before it goes live.

Invite your people

Share a join code for each location, team or classroom. Everyone joins from the InneRISE app, and a parent sets up each child.

Start a challenge

Pick one from the ready-made library or make your own, set the dates and rewards, and watch the standings move.
