What you can do

You're in charge, from your own phone.

Daily limits

Choose how much time your child gets on the apps that pull them in. When it's used up, those apps lock.

Bedtime

Distracting apps rest at bedtime and open again in the morning, every night, without a fight.

Requests for more time

Your child can ask for more time. You get a notification and answer with Allow or Not now.

Each child's day

A simple page for each child shows whether protections are working, with check-ins along the way.

Stays on

Your child can't switch protections off or remove InneRISE without you.

Two parents

Share Family Protection with another parent, so either of you can answer requests.

How setup works

A few minutes, once.

Your child needs their own iPhone. You'll need it in your hands for setup, and after that everything happens from your phone.

Install InneRISE on your child's iPhone

Follow the short setup checklist on your child's phone. It uses Apple's Screen Time, so app use stays on the phone.

Connect it to your phone

Link your child's iPhone to your own InneRISE account. This only happens once.

Set limits and bedtime

Pick the apps, the daily limit and bedtime. Change them any time from your phone.

Questions parents ask

Good to know

What does Family Protection cost?

Nothing. Family Protection is free.

Can InneRISE see what my child does on their phone?

No. Apple's Screen Time keeps app use on the phone. InneRISE never receives the names of the apps your child uses, the websites they visit, or how many minutes they spend on them.

Can strangers find my child?

No. Children can't be found by name, phone number, email or InneRISE code, except by the connections they already have.

What if my child really needs more time?

They tap to ask, and you get a notification on your phone. Answer it with Allow or Not now.
