What you can do

You're in charge,
from your own phone.

Daily limits

Pick the apps and a daily limit anywhere from 5 minutes to 12 hours. When it’s used up, those apps lock for the rest of the day.

Bedtime and school hours

Distracting apps rest at bedtime and during the school times and days you pick. Calls and messages still work, and bedtime ends on time by itself.

Requests for more time

Your child taps to ask. You get a notification and choose the minutes, up to 2 hours, or Not now with a short note.

Each phone’s status

See whether each phone is protected and when it last checked in. A phone that hasn’t checked in for 2 hours shows Not reporting.

Stays on

Your child can’t delete InneRISE without the Screen Time passcode, change its settings, or get around it by reinstalling. Need a new passcode? Set one from your own phone.

Two parents

A second parent can link to your child’s phone after you allow it, so either of you can answer requests.

How setup works

A few minutes, once.

Your child needs their own iPhone. You’ll need it in your hands for setup. After that, everything happens from your phone.

Install InneRISE on your child’s iPhone

In setup, choose “For my child” and approve with your Apple Account through Family Sharing. It uses Apple’s Screen Time, so app use stays on the phone.

Link it to your phone

On your child’s phone, go to Family Protection, then Link to a parent, and type your InneRISE code. You’ll get an invitation on your phone to accept. Linking is free.

Set limits, bedtime and school hours

Pick the apps, the daily limit, bedtime and school hours. Change them any time from your phone.

Questions parents ask

Good to know

What does Family Protection cost?

Nothing. Family Protection is free.

Can InneRISE see what my child does on their phone?

No. Apple's Screen Time keeps app use on the phone. InneRISE never receives the names of the apps your child uses, the websites they visit, or how many minutes they spend on them.

Can strangers find my child?

No. Children can't be found by name, phone number, email or InneRISE code, except by the connections they already have.

Does my child need an iPhone?

For now, yes. Family Protection uses Apple’s Screen Time, so your child needs their own iPhone. An Android version is in the works.

What if I forget the Screen Time passcode?

Reset it from your own phone. You set a new 6-digit passcode, and your child’s protections stay on the whole time.

What if my child really needs more time?

They tap to ask, and you get a notification on your phone. Pick how many minutes to give, or say Not now.
