How setup works
Daily limits and bedtime

Healthy limits.
Set from your phone.

Pick the apps and a daily limit anywhere from 5 minutes to 12 hours. When it’s used up, those apps lock for the rest of the day. Change it any time from your own phone.

  • Bedtime and school hours. Distracting apps rest at bedtime and during the school times and days you pick. Bedtime ends on time by itself.
  • Calls still work. Calls and messages keep working at bedtime and during school hours.
  • Each phone’s status. See whether each phone is protected and when it last checked in. A phone that hasn’t checked in for 2 hours shows Not reporting.
Requests for more time

They ask.
You decide.

When your child needs more time, they tap to ask. You get a notification on your phone and choose the minutes, up to 2 hours, or Not now with a short note.

  • Two parents. A second parent can link to your child’s phone after you allow it, so either of you can answer.
  • On your wrist. Answer from your Apple Watch too, and pick the minutes there.
  • Free. Family Protection is free, and so is linking.

Stays on

Protection your child
can’t switch off.

And your family’s privacy stays yours.

  • Your child can’t delete InneRISE without the Screen Time passcode.
  • They can’t change its settings or get around it by reinstalling.
  • Forgot the passcode? Set a new one from your own phone.
  • InneRISE never sees which apps or websites your child uses.
  • Strangers can’t find your child by name, number, email or code.
How setup works

A few minutes, once.

Install InneRISE on your child’s iPhone

In setup, choose “For my child” and approve with your Apple Account through Family Sharing. It uses Apple’s Screen Time, so app use stays on the phone.

Link it to your phone

On your child’s phone, go to Family Protection, then Link to a parent, and type your InneRISE code. You’ll get an invitation on your phone to accept. Linking is free.

Set limits, bedtime and school hours

Pick the apps, the daily limit, bedtime and school hours. Change them any time from your phone.

Questions parents ask

Good to know

What does Family Protection cost?

Nothing. Family Protection is free.

Can InneRISE see what my child does on their phone?

No. Apple's Screen Time keeps app use on the phone. InneRISE never receives the names of the apps your child uses, the websites they visit, or how many minutes they spend on them.

Can strangers find my child?

No. Children can't be found by name, phone number, email or InneRISE code, except by the connections they already have.

Does my child need an iPhone?

For now, yes. Family Protection uses Apple’s Screen Time, so your child needs their own iPhone. An Android version is in the works.

What if I forget the Screen Time passcode?

Reset it from your own phone. You set a new 6-digit passcode, and your child’s protections stay on the whole time.

What if my child really needs more time?

They tap to ask, and you get a notification on your phone. Pick how many minutes to give, or say Not now.
