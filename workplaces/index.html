Challenges & Competitions

Office against office.
Region against region.

Run a challenge for the whole company, or put locations, departments and teams head to head. Each region can play its own round, and the best go through to a company final.

  • Who takes part the whole company, one location, one department or one team.
  • Fair sides sides are ranked on the average per person, so a team of 6 can beat a team of 60.
  • Final rounds the top places from each region go through to a final.
  • What counts steps, active days, floors climbed, miles and more, from Apple Health and the fitness apps people already use.
  • Live standings in the app for everyone, and on the dashboard for you.
People & places

Set up once.
Grow from there.

Build InneRISE the way your company is really organized. Each place gets its own join code, so people land in the right spot from day one.

  • Your structure regions, districts, locations, departments and teams.
  • Roles administrators, location and department managers, team leads, challenge coordinators and a billing contact.
  • Bulk enrollment make join codes for many places at once and download them as a spreadsheet.
  • From your phone too administrators can handle people, codes and approvals in the InneRISE app.
Rewards

Rewards people
actually want.

Add rewards to any challenge: a team lunch, a chair massage, a certificate for everyone who takes part. Winners get a private code in the InneRISE app and show it at the front desk.

  • Who earns it everyone who reaches the target, the winning side, or the top few.
  • Approved first a reward counts only once an administrator approves it.
  • At the counter scan or type the code, check it, then confirm when you hand it over. Each code works once.
  • Never cash rewards are things and experiences, never cash, gift cards or balances.
Challenge & Rewards Library

Ready-made challenges.
Or make your own.

Start from InneRISE's library of ready-made challenges and rewards, or save your own and run it again next quarter. Not everything good shows up on a watch, so habit challenges work too.

  • Ready-made challenges and rewards from InneRISE, filtered by age group and how they count.
  • Your templates save a challenge once, then use it as it is or change it first.
  • Habit challenges for things like taking the stairs, a manager confirms the days. People can't mark themselves.

The pictures are real screens from the InneRISE dashboard, shown with sample organizations.

Privacy built in

Your people's health stays theirs.

Joining is each person's own choice, and they can leave any time.

  • Employers never see anyone's health records.
  • You see challenge results that people agree to share, nothing more.
  • Counts are rounded and small groups are hidden, so no one can be singled out.
  • We don't sell anyone's information or use tracking tools.
How it works

Up and running in three steps.
