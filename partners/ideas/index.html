Rewards honored everywhere

Verified, every time

Real rewards for real activity.

What counts

Steps, walking and running distance, cycling distance and floors from Apple Health, Garmin and Fitbit. App limits on each person’s own iPhone.

What never counts

Typed-in numbers. Dinner, reading or family time. Golf scores. Imported rides before they pass our checks.

How rewards are claimed

A one-time code your staff confirms at the counter. Your business never sees anyone’s health data.
