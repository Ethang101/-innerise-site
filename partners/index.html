● InneRISE for Partners

Reward the people who show up.

Gyms, studios, cafés and shops can sponsor InneRISE challenges and offer rewards people earn by finishing them. Then they come through your door to collect it.

Sponsored Challenges

Put your name on
a challenge people love.

Sponsor a challenge open to everyone on InneRISE in your area, like 10,000 steps on 20 days this month. Everyone who makes it earns your reward.

  • You choose the challenge, the dates, the area and the reward.
  • Real requirements verified steps, active days or a 30-day walking challenge, counted from people's phones and watches.
  • Reviewed first InneRISE reviews every challenge and reward before it goes live, and again after any change.
  • Ready-made start from a challenge in the InneRISE library, or write your own.
Rewards at your counter

Real rewards.
Never cash.

Offer a free class, a day pass, a discount or a free round. Winners show a QR code, a barcode or a short code from the InneRISE app, and your staff check it on any phone or computer.

  • You decide what the reward is, how many there are and how long they last.
  • No money involved no cash, gift cards or stored balances. You provide the reward yourself.
  • Once only checking a code never uses it up. Confirm does, and each code works one time.
  • Your codes or ours InneRISE can make the codes, or you can add codes from your own system.
  • Adults only offers are never shown to anyone under 18.
Performance

See what's working.
Never anyone's health.

Follow views, opens, joins and finishes day by day, for every challenge and offer you run.

  • What you see counts by day, and the results people choose to share once a challenge is final.
  • Small numbers hidden anything under 5 shows as "fewer than 5".
  • What you never see health or activity records, where people are, or anyone under 18.

How it works

Simple for you. Exciting for them.

Register your business

Add your locations and your team. InneRISE verifies every business first.

Create and send for review

Set up your challenge or offer, then send it to InneRISE. It goes live once it's approved.

Welcome them in person

Winners show their code at your counter. You see how your challenges are doing day by day.

Become an InneRISE partner.

Register your business and locations, and we'll verify it. Then create your first sponsored challenge or reward. Already a partner? Sign in to your dashboard.
