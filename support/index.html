Common questions

Answers

I didn't get my sign-in code

Codes come by email from support@innerise.app. Check that the address you typed is right, look in your spam or junk folder, and wait a minute. If it still hasn't arrived, ask for a new code.

How do I delete my account?
  1. Open InneRISE and go to the Protection tab.
  2. Scroll down to Account.
  3. Tap Delete Account and follow the steps.
How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are bought and managed through Apple. On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then tap Subscriptions and choose InneRISE.

How do I connect Garmin, Fitbit or another fitness app?
  1. Open InneRISE and go to the Protection tab.
  2. Tap Connected Apps.
  3. Choose your app and sign in to it.
Does InneRISE see which apps I use?

No. Apple's Screen Time keeps your app use on your phone. InneRISE never receives the names of the apps you use, the websites you visit, or how many minutes you spend on them.

Where can I read the privacy policy and terms?

Read the Privacy Policy and the Terms of Service.
