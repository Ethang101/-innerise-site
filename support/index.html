Your account

Account and sign-in

I didn’t get my sign-in code

Codes come by email from support@innerise.app. Check that the address you typed is right, look in your spam or junk folder, and wait a minute. If it still hasn’t arrived, ask for a new code.

How do I delete my account?
  1. Open InneRISE and go to the Protection tab.
  2. Scroll down to Account.
  3. Tap Delete Account and follow the steps.
How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are bought and managed through Apple. On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then tap Subscriptions and choose InneRISE.

Does InneRISE see which apps I use?

No. Apple’s Screen Time keeps your app use on your phone. InneRISE never receives the names of the apps you use, the websites you visit, or how many minutes you spend on them.

Where can I read the privacy policy and terms?

Read the Privacy Policy and the Terms of Service.

Family Protection

For parents

How do I set up Family Protection?
  1. Install InneRISE on your child’s iPhone. In setup, choose For my child and approve with your Apple Account through Family Sharing.
  2. On your child’s phone, go to Family Protection, then Link to a parent, and type your InneRISE code.
  3. Accept the invitation on your phone, then set limits, bedtime and school hours.

How Family Protection works

What if I forget the Screen Time passcode?

Reset it from your own phone. You set a new 6-digit passcode, and your child’s protections stay on the whole time.

Does my child need an iPhone?

For now, yes. Family Protection uses Apple’s Screen Time, so your child needs their own iPhone. An Android version is in the works.

What does Family Protection cost?

Nothing. Family Protection is free, and so is linking.

Using InneRISE

Challenges, apps and limits

How do I connect Garmin, Fitbit or another fitness app?
  1. Open InneRISE and go to the Protection tab.
  2. Tap Connected Apps.
  3. Choose your app and sign in to it.
Why don’t my steps count in a challenge?

Only activity recorded by a device counts, never typed-in entries. Apple Health (from your iPhone or Apple Watch), Garmin, and Fitbit or Pixel Watch count in challenges today. Ride with GPS, Polar, Withings and Oura show in the app but don’t count in challenges yet.

When does a challenge end?

Results lock in at noon the day after the last day, the same moment for everyone. A tie goes to the longer best streak.

How do I get more time when I’m locked out?

Tap Request More Time. Your Anchor gets a notification and answers Approve or Not now. A request lasts 15 minutes, and after that you can ask again.

Is there an Android version?

Not yet. InneRISE for Android and Galaxy Watch is coming soon.
